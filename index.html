Battery health, decoded

Know what that % means.

Battery health is unit-specific, especially on pre-owned phones. We make the check part of the conversation, not a surprise after you buy.

🔋

On an iPhone

Open Settings → Battery → Battery Health (or Battery Health & Charging). Check Maximum Capacity and Peak Performance Capability. On iPhone 15 and later, you can also review cycle count and first-use details.

✓

What to ask FITECH

Ask for a current screenshot or live check of the exact handset, plus confirmation of battery condition, parts/service history, Face ID, cameras and charging. We don’t publish a made-up battery percentage.

✳

Buying new or pre-owned?

For new sealed stock, ask how the shop verifies the device at opening. For pre-owned, check the displayed percentage in person and confirm warranty/return terms before paying.

Apple says iPhone 14 and earlier batteries are designed to retain 80% capacity at 500 complete charge cycles under ideal conditions; actual results vary with use and charging. Battery health is not a guarantee of remaining runtime.
